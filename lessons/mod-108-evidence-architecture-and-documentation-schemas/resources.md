# Resources — mod-108 AI Evidence Architecture and Documentation Schemas

Primary sources only. This module composes an enterprise evidence architecture on top of three convergent regulatory regimes and several standing bodies of primary literature: the EU AI Act (as the horizontal-regulation obligation source that fixes retention horizons, technical-documentation shape, and post-market monitoring / serious-incident timelines), SR 11-7 with the OCC 2011-12 companion (as the US federal banking model-risk-management documentation shape), and the ISO/IEC 42001 / 42005 / 42006 family (as the AI-management-system parent standards the evidence architecture conforms to, sitting on the ISO/IEC 27001 / 27002 information-security base). The card family (chapter 03) anchors to primary academic literature — Mitchell et al. on model cards, Gebru et al. on datasheets for datasets — and to the Hugging Face model-cards guide, Partnership on AI ABOUT ML, and the UK Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard. The supply-chain evidence stack (chapter 04) anchors to CycloneDX (including the ML-BOM profile), SPDX 3.0 (including the AI profile), SLSA, Sigstore, in-toto, and the NIST SSDF (SP 800-218). The regulator-facing artefact shapes (chapter 05) anchor to EU AI Act Articles 11 / 72 / 73, SR 11-7 documentation expectations, and the FDA's predetermined change-control plan (PCCP) guidance for AI/ML-enabled device software functions. The machine-readable catalog work (chapter 06) anchors to NIST OSCAL and its adjacent SP 800-53 / 800-53A companions. Sector overlays cited across chapters and exercises include HIPAA Security Rule §164.312(b), FDA 21 CFR Part 11, PCI DSS 4.x, the NAIC Model Bulletin on Use of AI by Insurers, Colorado SB 24-205, and NYC DCWP Local Law 144. Where a source is paywalled (ISO and ISO/IEC deliverables), the link resolves to the catalogue entry; the full normative text is behind ISO's licence and must be procured for authoritative use. Where a source is fast-moving (SPDX 3.0 AI profile status, ISO/IEC 42005 publication status, FDA PCCP guidance, OMB memoranda, sector-regulator bulletins, EU AI Act implementing acts), verify against the primary source at reading time and update the `<!-- needs-research: ... -->` markers in the chapter and exercise texts accordingly.
