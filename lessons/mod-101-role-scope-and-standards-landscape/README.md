# mod-101 — Senior AI Governance Architect Role Scope and the AI Standards Landscape

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

This module positions the senior AI governance / risk architect on the level ladder, reads the AI-specific standards stack as *architectural inputs* (not compliance checklists), and authors the engagement contracts with the roles immediately below and above. Every later module of the track — control library (mod-102), policy taxonomy (mod-103), cross-jurisdiction reconciliation (mod-104), AIMS (mod-105), risk taxonomy (mod-106), assurance (mod-107), evidence (mod-108), third-party (mod-109), post-market surveillance (mod-110), GRC-for-AI (mod-111), operating model (mod-112), and sector blueprints (mod-113) — hangs off the positioning and the reading discipline established here.

## Learning objectives

- Diagram this role's exact scope on the level ladder — what `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) and `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25) already own, what `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35), `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40), `head-of-ai-governance` (level 60), and `chief-ai-officer` (level 70) inherit or extend, and where the architectural design authority begins and ends.
- Read NIST AI RMF 1.0 (with Playbook + Generative AI Profile) as architectural inputs and locate each function (GOVERN / MAP / MEASURE / MANAGE) in the enterprise operating model.
- Read ISO/IEC 42001 (AIMS), 23894 (AI risk guidance), 42005 (impact assessment), 42006 (audit / certification bodies), 25059 (AI quality), 38507 (AI governance implications), and the 22989 / 23053 / TR 24028 vocabulary layer as the standards the architect writes against.
- Read NIST SP 800-53 rev5, SP 800-37 rev2, CSF 2.0, and OSCAL as the enterprise control-catalog + RMF process + machine-readable representation the AI control library composes with.
- Position ISO/IEC 27001, ISO 31000, ISO/IEC 27005, COSO ERM, COBIT 2019, and the IEEE 7000-series as sibling frameworks the AI architecture plugs into.
- Author the role's engagement contract with `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25) below, `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35) sideways-and-below, and `head-of-ai-governance` (level 60) above.
