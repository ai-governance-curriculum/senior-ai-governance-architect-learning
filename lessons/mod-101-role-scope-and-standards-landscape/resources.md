# Resources — mod-101 Role Scope and the AI Standards Landscape

Primary sources first. Paraphrases, vendor summaries, and blog posts are excluded — the reading discipline this module builds requires the standards themselves. If a link changes or a version supersedes, update in-repo rather than silently letting it drift.

## NIST AI RMF and Playbook

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (AI 100-1, Jan 2023)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf) — the core document.
- [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/AI_RMF_Knowledge_Base/Playbook) — suggested actions per sub-category (non-normative).
- [NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1, Jul 2024)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf) — Profile overlay for generative AI.
- [NIST AI Resource Center (AIRC)](https://airc.nist.gov/) — index for updates to the Framework, Playbook, and Profiles.
