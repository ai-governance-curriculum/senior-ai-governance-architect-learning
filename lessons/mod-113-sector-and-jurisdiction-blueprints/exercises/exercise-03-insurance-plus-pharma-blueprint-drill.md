# exercise-03: Insurance Plus Pharma Blueprint Drill

**Estimated effort:** 4 hours

## Objective

Produce two parallel sector adaptation records — one for an insurance carrier, one for a pharmaceutical company — that both instantiate the same reference architecture, and then produce the composition-map artefact that names the reference-architecture surfaces on which the two records compose (single control catalog, single provider register, single evidence-schema registry, single risk taxonomy, single AIMS with scope addenda). The two records are authored *in the same enterprise* — a holding company whose portfolio includes a P&C carrier with a Colorado-domiciled life-insurance affiliate and a biopharma subsidiary with GxP-scoped R&D, GMP-scoped manufacturing, and pharmacovigilance operations. The exercise proves that the mod-113 chapter-01 methodology composes across two very different sectors without forking the library, without duplicating the provider programme, and without producing two AIMS.

The correctness spine is the six invariants (I1..I6) from chapter 01 and the two failure modes chapter 01 designs against (the fork temptation; applicability-filter sprawl), together with the two sector-specific failure modes chapters 04 and 05 name (insurance: treating the NAIC bulletin as a single uniform obligation and missing per-state variance; pharma: importing SaMD-shaped controls onto drug-development AI). Every design choice in either record must be pinnable to one of the invariants (as enforcer) or to one of the failure modes (as defence). Do not reproduce the chapter YAML schematics verbatim; each record is an enterprise artefact and its shape reflects the enterprise's actual sectoral footprint.

- **Vendors** — the carrier uses at least one third-party AI system for claims-handling triage, one for fraud-detection scoring, and one that provides ECDIS-derived features into the Colorado life-insurance underwriting workflow.

**Part B — Consolidated Bio.** A mid-to-large biopharma subsidiary with:

- **Drug-discovery and clinical-trials AI** under GxP scope — GLP-scoped computational toxicology, GCP-scoped risk-based-monitoring and eligibility-screening AI (ICH E6(R3) applies <!-- needs-research: verify ICH E6(R3) adoption date and transposition status -->), with a GAMP 5 category mix of Cat 3, Cat 4, and Cat 5 systems.
- **Manufacturing AI** under GMP scope — a process-analytical-technology (PAT) model on a biologics manufacturing line and a computer-vision finished-product-inspection model, both feeding the batch-release decision under 21 CFR 211.22 and (for EU release) under Directive 2001/83/EC Article 48 Qualified Person authority.
- **Pharmacovigilance AI** under GVP scope — a case-triage model reading spontaneous adverse-event reports and a signal-detection model running across the safety database, with EMA GVP Module I QPPV accountability preserved.
- **Regulatory activity** — an active IND supporting a Phase 3 programme, a pending NDA/BLA planned for the next 18 months, and an ongoing MAA in the EU; AI use in each of discovery, non-clinical, clinical, CMC, and post-market will be referenced in the submission dossiers.
- **A combination-product boundary case** — a drug-eluting device programme where lead-centre determination (21 CFR Part 3) is pending; the AI-driven dosing controller sits at the CDER/CDRH boundary and requires interim co-governance with a hypothetical SAR-HEALTH.

Both subsidiaries operate under a **single enterprise AIMS**, a **single enterprise control catalog** (mod-102), a **single third-party provider register** (mod-109), a **single enterprise risk taxonomy and appetite statement** (mod-106), and a **single ai-governance-council** (mod-112). The exercise's central architectural claim is that this is coherent — that the six invariants hold across both sectors and that the reference architecture composes cleanly.

## Starter guidance

Draft the composition map first, before either sector-adaptation record. The two records look independent when read side by side, and drafting them independently is the fastest route to duplicating the provider evidence, the risk-taxonomy severity bands, and the AIMS scope-statement pattern. The composition map forces the shared reference-architecture surfaces to be named up front; each sector record then extends those surfaces rather than reinventing them. If you find yourself drafting a provider register entry for an ECDIS vendor in the insurance record and a separate provider register entry for the same vendor in the pharma record, you have already failed I1 and the mod-109 single-provider-register discipline.

The state-DOI response playbook is the insurance-side artefact most often catching enterprises unprepared, and it is worth drafting before the insurance sector-adaptation record's evidence-contract extensions are finalised — the playbook exposes which evidence artefacts the state DOI actually requests, and that exposure sharpens which per-obligation line items the record needs. The Colorado Testing-Methodology filing is the highest-visibility case, but the majority of state-DOI examinations use the non-Colorado adopting-state pattern, and the playbook must handle both branches without a forked evidence pipeline.

The eCTD Module 3/4 rendering specification is the pharma-side artefact most likely to be delivered late in the enterprise programme, and its lateness is expensive — an NDA/BLA/MAA whose AI-system documentation is not eCTD-shaped incurs FDA information-request cycles that add months to review. The renderer's authoring discipline is the same as any mod-108 render path — author once, render many — but the specificity of the eCTD module structure means the renderer's registration must be precise. If the renderer emits the model card into a wrong sub-module, the reviewer's screening will bounce the submission before substantive review begins.

The CSV/CSA composition-pattern choice for the pharma record is the single most consequential architectural choice in the exercise. Pattern 1 (CSV/CSA as evidence input to the mod-107 gate) is safer for enterprises whose PQS is not yet digitised; Pattern 2 (co-composed validation review) is more efficient for enterprises with a modernised PQS; Pattern 3 (gate as sole assurance forum) is an anti-pattern that will fail every GMP inspection and every FDA information request touching validation. State the choice and defend it; do not equivocate.

The filter-parsimony discipline (chapter 01 failure mode 2) is the discipline the exercise is easiest to fail on. Between the two records the applicability filter acquires at least four sector-added dimensions (`addressee_scope` NAIC state values; `covered-line-of-business`; `predicate-rule-scope`; `gamp-5-category`). Each addition must be defended — if `covered-line-of-business` can be folded into `addressee_scope` as a compound, do so; if `gamp-5-category` can be folded into an existing risk-tier dimension, do so. If not, state the budget increase and defend it at the composition-map level.
