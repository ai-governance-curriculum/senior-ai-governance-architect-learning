# Resources — mod-107 AI Assurance Architecture and Audit Readiness

Primary sources only. This module composes the enterprise AI assurance architecture on top of several standing bodies of work: the IIA Three Lines Model (as the parent governance-shape reference), the ISO/IEC 42001 family and ISO/IEC 42006 (as the AIMS certification-body composition), SR 11-7 and the OCC 2011-12 companion (as the US federal banking validation-vs-development independence reference), the EU AI Act and its post-market monitoring and serious-incident obligations, the ForHumanity IAAIS framework, the BABL AI Algorithmic Bias Auditor programme and the NYC Local Law 144 ecosystem, and NIST SP 800-37 with the SP 800-53 / 800-53A companions (as the US federal process-shape reference). Where a source is paywalled (ISO and ISO/IEC deliverables), the link resolves to the catalogue entry; the full normative text is behind ISO's licence and must be procured for authoritative use. Where a source is fast-moving (ForHumanity IAAIS revisions, NIST publications, EU AI Act implementing acts, sector-regulator guidance), verify against the primary source at reading time and update the `<!-- needs-research: ... -->` markers in the chapter and exercise texts accordingly.

## IIA Three Lines Model — the parent governance-shape reference (chapters 01, 04, 06; all exercises)

- [The IIA — The Three Lines Model (2020)](https://www.theiia.org/en/content/position-papers/2020/the-iia-three-lines-model/) — the 2020 update to the older Three Lines of Defence position paper. Reads the model as *specialisation of independence* rather than as a hierarchy of quality control. Chapter 01 uses this as the parent shape reference; every chapter after composes onto it.
- [The IIA — International Professional Practices Framework (IPPF)](https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/) — the professional-practices framework for internal audit. The 2024 update (published January 2024, effective January 2025) revised the standards structure. <!-- needs-research: verify the current IPPF publication status and the effective date of the 2024 revision as adopted; the IIA has moved through revisions and readers should confirm which version is authoritative at reading time. --> Chapter 04's six-phase engagement procedure and the audit-artefact contract are IPPF-shaped.
- [The IIA — Practice Guides (member-access)](https://www.theiia.org/en/content/practice-guides/) — the practice-guide series that translates the IPPF Standards into implementation guidance. Referenced by chapter 04 for the engagement-procedure and reporting-shape material.

## ISO/IEC 42001 and ISO/IEC 42006 — the AIMS certification-body composition (chapters 01, 02, 04, 05, 07; all exercises)

- [ISO/IEC 42006:2025 — Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Requirements for bodies providing audit and certification of artificial intelligence management systems (catalogue entry)](https://www.iso.org/standard/44637.html) <!-- needs-research: verify the current 42006 publication status, edition, and effective date at reading time; the standard was in final-approval stages during 2025 and its exact publication metadata should be confirmed. --> — the ISO/IEC standard specifying requirements for the certification body that audits an AIMS. ## How to use this list

- **When authoring the three-lines architecture** (exercise 01): the IIA Three Lines Model (2020) is the parent shape; SR 11-7 and OCC 2011-12 are the AI-specific independence discipline for the bank scenario; ISO/IEC 42006 is the audit-body competence composition. The frontier-lab framework URLs feed the stretch-goal comparable-enterprise reference.
- **When authoring the pre-deployment gate charter** (exercise 02): ISO/IEC 42001 Clause 8 anchors the operational-planning-and-control obligation; AI Act Articles 9–15 anchor the EU-scope evidence overlays; SR 11-7 anchors the bank-scenario overlays; FDA SaMD guidance anchors the healthcare-scenario overlays.
- **When authoring the ongoing-assurance programme** (exercise 03): AI Act Article 72 (post-market monitoring) and Article 73 (serious-incident notification) anchor the post-deployment-obligation discipline; SR 11-7 anchors the bank-scenario ongoing-validation cadence; FDA predetermined change-control plan guidance anchors the healthcare-scenario change discipline; Colorado SB 24-205 (as its rulemaking lands) anchors the state-level regulatory-change worked scenario.
- **When authoring the third-line audit programme** (exercise 04): the IIA IPPF is the process discipline; ISO/IEC 42006 is the audit-body competence baseline; ForHumanity IAAIS is the independence-and-domain-criteria composition; BABL AI is the specialist bias-audit composition; NIST AI 600-1 and the US / UK AISI publications are the evaluation-methodology inputs the competence programme tracks.
- **When authoring the external-audit interface** (exercise 05): each in-scope provider has a distinct reference — ISO/IEC 42001 / 42006 / 17021-1 / IAF MLA for the certification body; SR 11-7 for the federal banking supervisor; NAIC Model Bulletin for the state insurance department; AI Act Articles 43 / 72 / 73 / 74 / 99 / 101 for the EU market surveillance authority and notified body; FDA SaMD guidance for the FDA reviewer; NYC LL144 for the bias auditor; PCAOB / ISAE 3402 / 3000 for the statutory financial auditor.
- **When authoring the coordination contracts** (exercise 06): SR 11-7's validation-vs-development discipline anchors the evaluation-engineer coordination; ISO/IEC 42001 Clause 7 (Support) anchors the analyst role-scope contract's competence and documented-information sections.
- **When composing with the RMF process shape** (chapter 07 references in any exercise): SP 800-37 for the seven-step composition; SP 800-53 for the control-catalog reference at Select; SP 800-53A for the assessment-procedures reference at Assess; NIST AI 100-1 and AI 600-1 for the AI-specific extensions.
