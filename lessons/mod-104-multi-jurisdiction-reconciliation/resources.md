# Resources — mod-104 Cross-Jurisdiction Requirement Reconciliation Architecture

Primary sources only. This module reads regulatory instruments as architectural inputs — the URLs below are the publisher-hosted canonical locations for the statutes, memoranda, standards, and guidance the chapters and exercises reference. Where a source is fast-moving (bill status; OMB memorandum revisions; JTC 21 work-item progression), verify against the primary source at reading time and update the `<!-- needs-research: ... -->` markers in the chapter and exercise texts accordingly. Where a source is paywalled (ISO/IEC standards), the link resolves to the catalogue entry with the title, publication year, and abstract sufficient for architectural framing; the full text is behind ISO's licence.

## The EU AI Act and companion instruments (chapter 02)

- [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the EU AI Act) — consolidated Official Journal text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) — the primary source. Every article citation in chapter 02 and exercise-01 resolves against this text.
- [European Commission — AI Act policy page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai) — official summaries, implementation timeline, and delegated / implementing act status tracker.
- [European AI Office](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office) — the Commission body responsible for GPAI oversight, Article 56 code-of-practice facilitation, and coordination with national competent authorities.
- [EU AI Act — Annex III use-case list (as published in the Regulation)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) — high-risk use-case enumeration; referenced by exercise-01's Article 6-7 mapping.

## Cross-cutting international sources (chapter 03)

- [Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) — consolidated Official Journal text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) — Article 22 (automated individual decision-making) is the archetype cross-cutting AI-facing obligation.
- [Council of Europe Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights, Democracy and the Rule of Law](https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence/the-framework-convention-on-artificial-intelligence) — opened for signature 5 September 2024. Signatory list and text.
- [OECD AI Principles (adopted 2019, updated May 2024)](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles) — the five values-based principles and five recommendations to governments.
- [OECD Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence (OECD/LEGAL/0449)](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0449) — the formal legal instrument text.
- [UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence (2021)](https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics) — adopted November 2021 by all 193 UNESCO member states. Includes RAM (Readiness Assessment Methodology) and EIA (Ethical Impact Assessment) links.
- [G7 Hiroshima Process — International Guiding Principles and Code of Conduct for Organizations Developing Advanced AI Systems](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/hiroshima-process-international-guiding-principles-advanced-ai-system) — October 2023 publication.
