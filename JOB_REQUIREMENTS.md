## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical task domains for a Senior AI Governance / Risk Architect (level 50, AI Governance family) from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json → authoritative_references`](.aicg/job-requirements.json).
2. Ran the first exercised WebSearch / WebFetch pass against Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby / Workday / SmartRecruiters / Breezy / builtin.com / ai-governance-jobs.com aggregator + employer careers pages between 2026-07-12 and 2026-08-06. Sampled 26 architect-shaped postings across US, UK, Ireland, Canada, India, Portugal, and Hungary. 24 fall in the last-90-day window; 2 are retained window-adjacent for context.
3. Mapped each posting's verbatim required- and preferred-qualification bullets to (a) the existing requirement themes, (b) the module that already owns that theme, and (c) any candidate net-new theme.
4. Computed per-theme frequency as `postings-mentioning-theme / 24 in-window postings`.
5. Applied the continuity-bias rule from the research packet: propose net-new curriculum content only when a theme's frequency reaches ≥ 0.30, ≥ 3 distinct postings cite it, AND no existing module / exercise / project can absorb it. Three candidate net-new themes were evaluated (agentic-AI governance at architect scope, model-inventory / use-case-intake pipeline design at architect scope, cross-audience communication as a stand-alone module); all three either fall below the frequency threshold at strict architect-scope filtering or are already covered by an existing module. No additions triggered this cycle.
6. Applied the **ownership rule** — assign primary coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the skill; higher-level tracks link back rather than duplicate. In particular, deferred:
   - *Down* to [`ai-governance-analyst`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-governance-analyst-learning) (level 15) for operational analyst legwork (intake, inventory, framework-crosswalk drafting, first-draft impact assessments, model-card authoring at analyst-tier, control-tracking, regulatory tracking, reading eval / audit evidence). Confirmed by post-01-occ, post-08-lexisnexis, post-15-nbc (analyst-tier deliverables inside otherwise-architect-shaped postings).
   - *Down* to [`ai-risk-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (level 25) for the hands-on engineering craft of AI risk (harm-model authoring, red-team / adversarial-ML / fairness / privacy / guardrail engineering, quantification, MRM engineering, incident RCA). Confirmed by post-06-hartford, post-11-keybank, post-15-nbc, post-25-optum (Python / LangChain / SDLC-shaped requirements inside architect postings — architect assumes review-and-sign-off depth, does not re-teach).
   - *Sideways-and-down* to [`ai-evaluation-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (level 35, Governance family) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology depth. Confirmed by post-06-hartford, post-11-keybank, post-14-empower (LLM evaluation / validation-methods-shaped requirements — architect designs the assurance system that peer executes inside).
   - *Up-sideways* to [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) / [`ai-infra-security`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning) (level 35) for deep ML/AI security at platform scale.    - *Up* to [`agentic-safety-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/agentic-safety-engineer-learning) (level 40) for frontier-agent red-team methodology and dangerous-capability evaluation. Confirmed by post-02-anthropic (frontier-lab compliance) and post-12-amgen (agentic AI governance) — 2 postings, below threshold, appropriately deferred up.

Confirmed by post-02-anthropic (Board / Audit Committee / regulator engagement) — mostly out-of-scope for this level-50 role.
